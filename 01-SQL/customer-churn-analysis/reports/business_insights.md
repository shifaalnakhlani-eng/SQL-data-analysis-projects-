# Business Insights — IBM Telco Customer Churn

## Executive summary
- Total customers: **7,043**
- Churned customers: **1,869**
- Retained customers: **5,174**
- Overall churn rate: **26.54%**
- Retention rate: **73.46%**
- Average monthly charge: **64.76** (currency not specified here)

## Findings
1. Month-to-month contracts have the highest churn: **42.71%** (1,655 of 3,875), compared with 11.27% for one-year and 2.83% for two-year contracts.
2. Churn is concentrated among newer customers: **47.44%** in the 0–12 month group (1,037 of 2,186), compared with 9.51% in the 49–72 month group (213 of 2,239).
3. Electronic-check customers have **45.29%** churn (1,071 of 2,365), versus 15.24% for automatic credit-card payments.
4. Fiber-optic customers have **41.89%** churn (1,297 of 3,096), compared with 18.96% for DSL and 7.40% for customers without internet service.
5. Customers without tech support have **41.64%** churn (1,446 of 3,473), compared with 15.17% for customers with tech support.

## Recommendations to investigate
- Review onboarding and early-life support for customers in their first year.
- Investigate the value proposition and customer experience for month-to-month customers.
- Examine billing friction and customer preferences associated with electronic-check payments; do not assume payment method causes churn.
- Review fiber-optic service quality, installation experience, pricing and complaint data.
- Compare technical-support access and satisfaction with churn patterns before proposing an intervention.

## Limitations
These are descriptive associations in a static sample, not causal findings. Consider segment size alongside churn rate. The dataset has no cancellation reasons or longitudinal monthly history. Monthly charges associated with churned customers are not confirmed revenue lost.
